Sora Quiz

The Sora Quiz is an interactive web application designed to provide users with a fun and engaging trivia experience. Users can answer a series of trivia questions, select their answers from multiple choices, and receive instant feedback based on their selections. The trivia questions are categorized into three difficulty levels—Easy, Medium, and Hard—to ensure that players of all knowledge levels can enjoy the game.

  • Questions Retrieval.
  • Different Difficulty Levels.
  • User Interface.
  • Dynamic Question Display.
  • Feedback System.

The main objective is to provide a fun and educational experience for users by offering an easy-to-use trivia quiz. It serves as both an engaging pastime and a tool for learning new facts in an entertaining format.

Sora Pong

Sora Pong is a classic arcade reimagined — play against a smart AI opponent or challenge a friend in local two-player mode. Choose your game mode, set a winning score limit, and battle it out.

  • Player vs AI with adaptive difficulty.
  • Local Player vs Player mode.
  • Endless or score-limit game modes.
  • Keyboard controls — responsive and smooth.

Controls — vs AI: ↑ / ↓ arrow keys.
Controls — vs Player: Player 1 uses W / S  |  Player 2 uses ↑ / ↓.
